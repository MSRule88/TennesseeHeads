Find Your Music - Find Your People

Welcome to Tennessee Heads, the home of Tennessee's independent music and arts community! Whether you are a Deadhead, Jam Band enthusiast, Bluegrass fanatic, Rock devotee, Americana aficionado or just love good music and are in the Middle Tennessee area this is the place for you! Take a trip through our website, you sure don't want to miss out on everything that's going on!

Tennessee Heads Community Calendar

Keep up to date on all the good music events with our shared Google calendar!

You can view the events on our website or download the Google calendar app to access it from your phone and help us keep the calendar up to date! Everyone with access to the calendar via the app is given full rights to add their own events, a true community effort!
