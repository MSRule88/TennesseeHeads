Instructions for viewing the Calendar on your phone

Android and iPhone Users

Download the Google Calendar App and sign in with your Google account

Instructions for iPhone Users to sync with the iPhone Calendar app

Open Settings: Tap the Settings app on your iPhone.

Go to Calendar: On newer iOS versions, tap Apps, then select Calendar. On older iOS versions, scroll down and tap Calendar directly.

Add Account: Tap Calendar Accounts (or Accounts). Tap Add Account. Select Google.

Sign In: Enter your Google email address and password (or follow the two-step verification prompt if enabled).

Enable Calendar Sync: Once signed in, make sure the toggle for Calendars is turned on for that account. You can turn off Mail, Contacts, or Notes if you only want to sync the calendar.

View Events in Apple Calendar: Open the native Calendar app on your iPhone. Tap Calendars at the bottom of the screen.

Check the box next to your Google account calendars so they appear.
